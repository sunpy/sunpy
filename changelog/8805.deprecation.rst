Deprecated the ``dtype``, ``ndim``, ``min``, ``max``, ``mean`` and ``std`` attributes/methods of `~sunpy.map.GenericMap` in favour of working with ``GenericMap.data`` directly.
``map.dtype`` and ``map.ndim`` become ``map.data.dtype`` and ``map.data.ndim``.
For the statistics, use :func:`numpy.nanmin`, :func:`numpy.nanmax`, :func:`numpy.nanmean` and :func:`numpy.nanstd` on ``map.data``.
