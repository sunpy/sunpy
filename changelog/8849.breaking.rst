When a FITS file stores its data as scaled integers, `astropy.io.fits` applies the
``BSCALE``/``BZERO`` scaling as the data is read.
The metadata of the resulting `~sunpy.map.GenericMap` now reflects that: ``BSCALE`` and
``BZERO`` are no longer present and ``BITPIX`` describes the scaled data rather than the
integers on disk.
Previously these keywords were carried over unchanged even though they no longer
described the data they were paired with, which could lead to the scaling being applied
a second time.
Files that do not use ``BSCALE``/``BZERO`` are unaffected.
